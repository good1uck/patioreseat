This page is part of our [Brown Jordan guide](/brands/brown-jordan/). **Calcutta** is Brown Jordan’s faux-bamboo, Chinese Chippendale-style collection in cast aluminum. It is still in the current lineup, but it has a long second life on the resale market, and that is what makes cushion orders tricky. A Calcutta chair bought at an estate sale and a Calcutta chair bought from a dealer this year can be different frames, so their cushions may not be the same size.

## What Calcutta is

Brown Jordan’s [Calcutta collection page](https://www.brownjordan.com/products/collection/cushion/calcutta) (checked November 25, 2026, America/New_York) says the design is “inspired by the design themes of Chippendale furniture” and “features cast bamboo patterns on the seat and back, while tapered legs and a lower x-stretcher continue the exotic bamboo pattern.” It says the collection is “appropriate for indoor formal dining as well as outdoor use.” The same page says designer Hall Bradley made the original prototypes by bending live bamboo stalks into place and then casting those parts in metal.

So if your furniture is aluminum, looks like bamboo lattice, and has an X-shaped stretcher low between the legs, Calcutta is worth checking.

## Two generations: the 1967 original and the reintroduction

Brown Jordan’s own sources agree on the origin but not on the date of the reintroduction:

- The [All Collections page](https://www.brownjordan.com/products/collection) says Calcutta was “originally designed by Hall Bradley in 1967 and reintroduced on a grander scale by John Caldwell in 1987.”
- The 2024 [All Collections catalog (PDF)](https://platform.brownjordan.com/media/pdf/All%20Collections%20Catalog.pdf) (p. 11) uses the same sentence but says “in 2007.”

We can’t tell you which year is right. What matters for cushions is the phrase both sources use: reintroduced **“on a grander scale.”** Today’s pieces are credited to John Caldwell on every Calcutta product page, and Brown Jordan doesn’t say the current frames match the original 1967 ones. Aftermarket shops treat them as separate: Cushions USA lists “Calcutta” among current Brown Jordan cushion collections and “Calcutta, (old)” among its early collections ([betterthanbrownjordan.com](https://betterthanbrownjordan.com/), checked November 25, 2026).

Resale listings show how different the older pieces can be. These are sellers’ own measurements of vintage Calcutta pieces, not Brown Jordan figures, and we haven’t checked them:

- A [Modern Day Bids auction lot](https://www.moderndaybids.com/auction/lot/2cu7tqyn/vintage-brown-jordan-calcutta-outdoor-furnitu) lists a vintage armchair “with slatted back and seat” at 28" w × 26" d × 31.5" (excluding cushion), and a dining chair with a “woven seat” at 22" w × 20" d × 34.5" h, both sold with “original Brown Jordan black outdoor cushions with removable covers.”
- A [1stDibs listing](https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/building-garden/garden-furniture/vintage-brown-jordan-calcutta-faux-bamboo-aluminum-five-piece-patio-dining-set/id-f_50760072/) for a 1970s dining set says it comes with “the original vinyl seat cushions.”

Compare those with the current dining side chair, which Brown Jordan lists at 19.25" wide and 25.75" long (table below). If your set came from a resale listing, an older catalog, or a relative’s patio, assume it may be the older generation until someone confirms otherwise.

## The current Calcutta seating lineup

Brown Jordan files Calcutta under **Cushion** collections. These are the seating pieces on brownjordan.com on November 25, 2026. All of them use the item-number prefix **3510-**. (Calcutta tables use 3512- and 3513- numbers; they don’t take cushions.)

| [Dining Side Chair](https://www.brownjordan.com/calcutta-side-chair) | 3510-1000 | Side Chair |
| [Dining Arm Chair](https://www.brownjordan.com/calcutta-arm-chair) | 3510-2000 | — |
| [Lounge Chair](https://www.brownjordan.com/calcutta-lounge-chair) | 3510-6000 | — |
| [Ottoman](https://www.brownjordan.com/calcutta-ottoman) | 3510-6100 | — |
| [Loveseat](https://www.brownjordan.com/calcutta-loveseat) | 3510-6200 | — |
| [Sofa](https://www.brownjordan.com/calcutta-sofa) | 3510-6300 | — |
| [Adjustable Chaise w/Wheels](https://www.brownjordan.com/calcutta-adjustable-chaise-with-wheels) | 3510-7000 | Chaise Lounge w/ Wheels |

Two things to notice:

- **There is only one chaise.** Unlike [Venetian](/brands/brown-jordan/venetian/), which lists a chaise with and without wheels, current Calcutta has a single Adjustable Chaise with Wheels, and its item number is 3510-7000. Don’t assume a “-7700” wheeled version exists because another collection has one.
- **There are no sectional or swivel pieces** in the current lineup. If you have a Calcutta piece that isn’t in this table, see “Pieces that aren’t on the list” below.

What Brown Jordan says about the cushions themselves:

- The Lounge Chair page describes “plush upholstered deep seating cushions.” The Ottoman has “a plush upholstered deep cushion.” The Loveseat and Sofa pages mention “Suncloth fabric cushions.”
- Every seating page, including the two dining chairs, offers a fabric choice and a separate welt choice.
- The catalog (p. 13) says “cushions available in all Suncloth fabric and Versatex sling” and “contrasting welt available.” Brown Jordan doesn’t explain where Versatex sling would be used on a Calcutta piece. Ask the retailer before you order.

## Furniture covers are not cushion covers

Each Calcutta product page also recommends a matching **cover**, such as “Side Chair Cover,” “Arm Chair Cover,” “Lounge Chair/Motion Lounge Chair Cover,” “Ottoman Cover,” “Loveseat Cover,” “Sofa Cover,” and “Chaise Cover.” Brown Jordan sells these under Accessories → Covers. They are covers that go over the furniture to protect it, not replacement covers for your cushions. Make sure you don’t order the wrong one.

## Furniture dimensions are not cushion sizes

Each Calcutta product page lists the **furniture’s** dimensions: width (W), length (L), height (H), seat height (SH), arm height (AH), and seat depth (SD). From brownjordan.com on November 25, 2026:

| Piece | Item | W | L | H | SH | SD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dining Side Chair | 3510-1000 | 19.25" | 25.75" | 35.75" | 20" | 19" |
| Dining Arm Chair | 3510-2000 | 24.25" | 25.75" | 35.75" | 20" | 19" |
| Lounge Chair | 3510-6000 | 29" | 33.5" | 35.75" | 19" | 20.5" |
| Ottoman | 3510-6100 | 24.75" | 25.5" | 18.75" | 18.75" | — |
| Loveseat | 3510-6200 | 52.75" | 33.5" | 35.75" | 19" | 20.5" |
| Sofa | 3510-6300 | 76.5" | 33.5" | 35.75" | 19" | 20.5" |
| Adjustable Chaise w/Wheels | 3510-7000 | 29.25" | 82.5" | 42" | 19" | 48" |

The 2024 catalog prints the same widths, depths, and heights, except for one apparent misprint: it lists the loveseat as “D 33.5 • D 35.75,” where the second figure is probably the height.

Here is why you shouldn’t work out a cushion size from this table:

- **Seat depth is a frame measurement.** The lounge chair’s 20.5" seat depth tells you about the chair, not about how deep the seat cushion is, how thick it is, or how its corners are shaped.
- **It only covers the current generation.** None of these numbers apply to an older Calcutta frame (see above).

Measure the cushions you have with our [how-to-measure guide](/guides/how-to-measure/), and photograph the corners, the welt, the zipper, and any ties. If you have no cushions at all, make a paper template of the seat and photograph it beside a tape measure.

## What new Calcutta pieces cost (for context)

On November 25, 2026, brownjordan.com listed these “Starting at MSRP” prices for whole pieces of furniture, not replacement cushions: Dining Side Chair $2,495; Ottoman $2,795; Dining Arm Chair $2,895; Lounge Chair $4,895; Adjustable Chaise w/Wheels $7,995; Loveseat $8,495; Sofa $9,995. [Patio.com](https://www.patio.com/calcutta) listed the same regular prices that day.

Brown Jordan doesn’t publish replacement-cushion prices online, so ask a retailer for a quote. Prices change; check the live pages.

## How to order replacement cushions

1. **Find your item number.** If you have paperwork from a Brown Jordan retailer, look for a 3510- number. Give the retailer that number, not a description.
2. **If you don’t know what you have, get it identified.** Brown Jordan’s [FAQ](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/faq) (checked November 25, 2026) says you can email a photo of your furniture and your name to `bjcservice@brownjordan.com` to be told the collection name. For Calcutta, also ask whether your frame is the current version or an older one, and whether cushions for it can still be ordered.
3. **Order through a retailer.** The same FAQ says replacement cushions “can be purchased from your local Brown Jordan retailer” ([Locations](https://www.brownjordan.com/locations)). Replacement slings and strap go through a retailer or a local service technician ([Technician Locator](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/technician-locator)).
4. **If the retailer can’t help, measure and go custom.** See “If a retailer can’t help” below.

### Pieces that aren’t on the list

If your Calcutta piece isn’t in the table above, it may be an older or special-order piece. Don’t pick the nearest current piece and order its cushions. Get the piece identified first (step 2), then measure what you have.

## Fabric and warranty notes

The catalog says Calcutta cushions are available in all Suncloth fabric. Brown Jordan’s [Suncloth Fabric](https://www.brownjordan.com/materials/textiles/suncloth-fabric) page shows the current colorways, and the product pages offer a separate welt fabric. If you are matching cushions you already have, ask the retailer for the fabric name and number from your original order rather than matching by eye. The collection page shows one set with cushions in “pea green fabric and welting,” so say whether your cushions have a matching or contrasting welt. Our [Softscape guide](/brands/brown-jordan/softscape/) explains Suncloth Fabric vs. Suncloth Strap, and our [fabric guide](/guides/fabrics/) covers outdoor fabrics in general.

Older Calcutta sets were sold with other coverings; one resale listing above mentions original vinyl seat cushions. A replacement doesn’t have to copy the original material. For an outdoor set, choose an outdoor fabric and foam.

Brown Jordan’s residential warranty on its [Claims & Warranty page](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/claims-and-warranty) (checked November 25, 2026) covers fabric on cushions for five years if it “becomes unserviceable due to color or strength loss from normal usage and exposure conditions.” It doesn’t cover “costs associated with replacement of the fabric, including labor and installation.” If your Calcutta set is fairly new, check the warranty before you pay for replacements, and read the current page rather than relying on our summary. A vintage set is long past any warranty.

## If a retailer can’t help

If the official route can’t supply what you need (an older frame, a discontinued fabric, a single cushion, or a set bought second-hand), several aftermarket shops say they make cushions for Calcutta. None of them is Brown Jordan, and none published Calcutta prices when we checked on November 25, 2026. All of them quote on request:

- **[Cushions USA](https://betterthanbrownjordan.com/)** (Upland, California, per its [Heritage Cushions page](https://heritagecushions.com/brown-jordan-replacement-cushions/)) lists both current “Calcutta” and “Calcutta, (old)” and says it has most Brown Jordan templates, with no measurements required. It offers to identify collections from emailed photos.
- **[Online Replacement Cushions](https://www.onlinereplacementcushions.com/brown-jordan-replacement-cushions/)** lists Brown Jordan Calcutta replacement cushions. Its quote form asks for the collection name, the pieces, and a specific Sunbrella fabric, and recommends uploading photos.

“No measurements required” is a shop’s claim, not a guarantee. Because Calcutta has two generations, tell any shop whether yours is a vintage set or a current one, send photos, and keep your own measurements so you can check its sizes before it cuts. Most custom cushions are made to order and may not be returnable, so check the return policy first. A general custom shop can also cut cushions to your measurements. Tell them it is a Brown Jordan Calcutta piece on a cast-aluminum frame, and send photos of how each cushion sits and attaches.
