This page is part of our [Brown Jordan guide](/brands/brown-jordan/). **Parkway** is a John Caldwell design, and like [Fremont](/brands/brown-jordan/fremont/), [Pasadena](/brands/brown-jordan/pasadena/), and [Softscape](/brands/brown-jordan/softscape/), the one name covers several different builds. Parkway has more of them than most: a cushion group, a modular sectional group, and two different sling groups. Only two of the four take loose cushions, and one of the sling groups has been renamed. Work out which one you own before you order anything.

## One name, four builds

Brown Jordan’s [Parkway collection page](https://www.brownjordan.com/products/collection/multi-type/parkway) (checked November 24, 2026, America/New_York) says John Caldwell designed the collection in 2010 and that it “comes in three material translations, Parkway Sling, Parkway Cushion, and Parkway Flex,” and that “Caldwell later designed a Modular system.” It mentions “22 premier finish options.”

The 2024 [All Collections catalog (PDF)](https://platform.brownjordan.com/media/pdf/All%20Collections%20Catalog.pdf) gives a different year. Its Parkway Sling page says Caldwell “created the award-winning Parkway collection for Brown Jordan in 2006 and interpreted the design in Parkway Flex, Parkway Sling, Parkway Cushion, and Parkway Modular.” We can’t tell you which year is right. If you date your set from a receipt, don’t rule out Parkway just because the purchase came before 2010.

On brownjordan.com (checked November 24, 2026) the four groups are:

| Group | Loose cushions? | Item-number prefix | Pieces listed on brownjordan.com |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Parkway Cushion](https://www.brownjordan.com/products/collection/cushion/parkway-cushion) | **Yes** | 3470- | Dining Arm Chair, Lounge Chair, Motion Lounge Chair, Swivel Rocker, Ottoman, Loveseat, Sofa |
| [Parkway Modular](https://www.brownjordan.com/products/collection/cushion/parkway-modular) | **Yes** | 4560- | Corner, Center, and Quarter Round Sectionals; Sectional and Quarter Round Ottomans; Left Arm, Right Arm, and Armless Loveseats; Left Arm and Right Arm Sofas; Sofa |
| [Parkway Sling](https://www.brownjordan.com/products/collection/sling/parkway-sling) | No (sling seat) | 5330- | Sofa, Loveseat, Stacking Dining Arm Chair, Dining Side Chair, Arm Bar Stool, Armless Bar and Counter Stools, Lounge Chair, Motion Lounge Chair, Sand Chair, Ottoman, Flat Stacking Adjustable Chaise |
| [Parkway Framed Sling](https://www.brownjordan.com/products/collection/sling/parkway-framed-sling) (the catalog’s “Parkway Flex Sling”) | No (sling seat; see the chaise pad note below) | 3470- | Dining Arm Chair, Swivel Rocker, Bar Stool, Motion Lounge Chair, Ottoman, Stacking Chaise, Flat Adjustable Chaise w/Wheels |

### “Parkway Flex” is now “Parkway Framed Sling”

If you bought Parkway Flex, you won’t find a “Parkway Flex” group on the current site. Those pieces are now listed as **Parkway Framed Sling**. The web addresses still say “flex” (for example, the motion lounge chair lives at `/parkway-flex-sling-motion-lounge-chair`), and the item numbers match the catalog’s Parkway Flex Sling page: 3470-2200 arm chair, 3470-4700 swivel rocker, 3470-3500 bar stool, 3470-5300 motion lounge chair, 3470-5100 ottoman, 3470-7000 adjustable chaise with wheels, and 3470-7200 stacking chaise. The collection page describes the Flex motion lounge chair’s “supple Parabolic sling that flexes and offers support.”

## Which Parkway do you have?

### By the full item number, not just the last four digits

An invoice, packing slip, or spec sheet settles it, but read the **whole** number. Parkway has two traps:

1. **Parkway Cushion and Parkway Framed Sling (Flex) both use 3470-.** The cushion pieces are 3470-2000 (arm chair), 3470-4600 (swivel rocker), 3470-6000 (lounge chair), 3470-5200 (motion lounge chair), 3470-6100 (ottoman), 3470-6200 (loveseat), and 3470-6300 (sofa). The Framed Sling pieces are the 3470- numbers listed in the section above. So “3470” alone doesn’t mean you have cushions.
2. **The last four digits repeat across groups.** The Parkway Cushion motion lounge chair is **3470-5200**, and the Parkway Sling motion lounge chair is **5330-5200**. The Framed Sling ottoman is **3470-5100**, and the Parkway Sling ottoman is **5330-5100**. A shop that only hears “5200” or “5100” could quote the wrong part.

| If your number starts with… | And ends with… | You have |
|---|---|---|
| 3470- | -2000, -4600, -5200, -6000, -6100, -6200, -6300 | Parkway Cushion (loose cushions) |
| 3470- | -2200, -3500, -4700, -5100, -5300, -7000, -7200 | Parkway Framed Sling / Flex (sling) |
| 4560- | any seating number | Parkway Modular (loose cushions) |
| 5330- | any | Parkway Sling (sling) |

These are the numbers we found on brownjordan.com and in the 2024 catalog on November 24, 2026. Brown Jordan doesn’t present them as a decoding rule, and older pieces may not be on either list.

### By the piece type

- **A sectional, corner piece, or quarter-round piece is Parkway Modular.** No other Parkway group lists them.
- **A dining side chair, sand chair, or counter stool is Parkway Sling.** These appear only in that group on the current site.
- **A loveseat or sofa could be Cushion, Modular, or Sling.** Check the seat. A sling seat stretched in the frame is Parkway Sling. Loose seat cushions on a 35"-deep frame (the depth listed for every Modular piece) point to Modular. Loose cushions on a frame listed at 34.5" deep point to Parkway Cushion.
- **A chaise is never Parkway Cushion.** Both Parkway chaises are in the sling groups. (See the chaise pad note below.)

### By photo

Brown Jordan’s [FAQ](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/faq) (checked November 24, 2026) says you can email a photo of your furniture and your name to `bjcservice@brownjordan.com`, and the company “would be happy to provide you with the name of your Brown Jordan collection.” Ask them to name the group (Parkway Cushion, Modular, Sling, or Framed Sling) and, if they can, the item number.

## Cushion or sling? What is actually worn

Brown Jordan’s FAQ treats these as different jobs. Replacement cushions “can be purchased from your local Brown Jordan retailer,” while replacement slings and strap “can be purchased from a Brown Jordan retailer or a local service technician” ([FAQ](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/faq); [Locations](https://www.brownjordan.com/locations); [Technician Locator](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/technician-locator)).

| What is worn | What it probably is | Where to go |
|---|---|---|
| A seat or back pad that lifts off the frame: faded, flattened, torn, or a failed zipper | A **loose cushion** (Parkway Cushion 3470-2000/-4600/-5200/-6000/-6100/-6200/-6300, or Parkway Modular 4560-) | A Brown Jordan retailer, with the collection name, item number, and fabric if you know it |
| Fabric stretched tight across the frame that is torn, sagging, or faded | A **sling** (Parkway Sling 5330-, or Parkway Framed Sling / Flex) | A Brown Jordan retailer or a service technician, not a cushion shop |

Owners do run into the sling job. One r/Westchester poster asked where to get “a few brown Jordan patio chairs/chaise lounges repaired” because “the slings have torn” ([Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Westchester/comments/1dgumjl/patio_repair_sling/)). That is re-slinging work. New cushions won’t fix it.

### Two catalog notes to ask your retailer about

- **Versatex sling on cushion pieces.** The 2024 catalog says Parkway Cushion is “available in all Suncloth fabric and Versatex sling,” and says the same of Parkway Modular. Brown Jordan’s pages don’t say where a sling sits on a cushion piece. Before you order, take the cushions off and photograph the frame. Then ask the retailer whether your piece has a sling under the cushions and whether that sling also needs replacing. Brown Jordan’s [Versatex Sling](https://www.brownjordan.com/materials/textiles/versatex-sling) page lists the current sling colors.
- **The Flex chaise pad.** The catalog lists a **3470-7001 Chaise Pad** next to the Parkway Flex adjustable chaise with wheels (3470-7000), with the note “Suncloth fabrics are available to order on the chaise pad.” If your sling chaise has a separate pad on top, that pad is a cushion order and the sling under it is a sling order. We didn’t find the chaise pad on the current product page, so ask a retailer whether it can still be ordered.

## Parkway Modular: order by piece, not by “left” or “right”

Modular is where replacement orders most often go wrong, because several pieces are mirror images. On brownjordan.com (checked November 24, 2026):

Brown Jordan’s pages don’t say which side “left arm” refers to. Rather than guess, give the retailer the item number from your paperwork. If you don’t have it, photograph the whole sectional from the front so they can see where each arm is. A few other details from the product copy:

- The Quarter Round Sectional page says it has “tailored cushions on each piece with joining devices included.”
- The Sectional Ottoman and Quarter Round Ottoman pages say their cushions “can be altered to accept welting.” Ask whether yours has welt before you match a replacement.
- The product copy calls Modular a “ten-piece collection,” but the collection page lists 11 seating pieces (plus tables). Go by the piece name and number, not the count.

## Furniture dimensions are not cushion sizes

Every Parkway product page lists frame dimensions: width (W), length (L), height (H), seat height (SH), arm height (AH), and seat depth (SD). These describe the **furniture**, not the cushion. For Parkway Cushion, from brownjordan.com on November 24, 2026:

| Piece | Item | W | L | H | SH | SD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Dining Arm Chair](https://www.brownjordan.com/parkway-cushion-arm-chair) | 3470-2000 | 28.5" | 30.5" | 33" | 20" | 20" |
| [Swivel Rocker](https://www.brownjordan.com/parkway-cushion-swivel-rocker) | 3470-4600 | 28.5" | 30.5" | 33" | 20" | 20" |
| [Lounge Chair](https://www.brownjordan.com/parkway-cushion-lounge-chair) | 3470-6000 | 33.25" | 34.5" | 33" | 19.5" | 18.25" |
| [Motion Lounge Chair](https://www.brownjordan.com/parkway-cushion-motion-lounge-chair) | 3470-5200 | 34.75" | 34.5" | 33" | 19.5" | 22" |
| [Ottoman](https://www.brownjordan.com/parkway-cushion-ottoman) | 3470-6100 | 33.25" | 26" | 19" | 19" | — |
| [Loveseat](https://www.brownjordan.com/parkway-cushion-loveseat) | 3470-6200 | 59.25" | 34.5" | 33" | 19.5" | 22" |
| [Sofa](https://www.brownjordan.com/parkway-cushion-sofa) | 3470-6300 | 85.25" | 34.5" | 33" | 19.5" | 22" |

Spec tables aren’t always consistent. The lounge chair is listed with an 18.25" seat depth while the motion lounge chair, loveseat, and sofa on the same frame depth are listed at 22". The catalog doesn’t print seat depth at all. On the Framed Sling motion lounge chair, the website lists a 24.5" height while the catalog prints 34.5". Don’t work out a cushion size from these numbers. Measure the cushion you have with our [how-to-measure guide](/guides/how-to-measure/), and photograph the corners, the welt, the zipper, and any ties.

## Fabric and warranty notes

The catalog says Parkway Cushion and Parkway Modular are available in all Suncloth fabric. Brown Jordan’s [Suncloth Fabric](https://www.brownjordan.com/materials/textiles/suncloth-fabric) page shows the current colorways. Our [Softscape guide](/brands/brown-jordan/softscape/) explains the difference between Suncloth Fabric (for cushions) and Suncloth Strap, and our [fabric guide](/guides/fabrics/) covers outdoor fabrics in general. If you are matching cushions you already have, ask the retailer for the fabric name and number from your original order rather than matching by eye.

Brown Jordan’s residential warranty on its [Claims & Warranty page](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/claims-and-warranty) (checked November 24, 2026) covers “Fabric on Cushions, Slings, Umbrellas and Furniture Covers” for 5 years (limited) if the fabric “becomes unserviceable due to color or strength loss from normal usage and exposure conditions.” Failures in “stitching, zippers, sling rails, cording, buttons, cushion foam or other areas of workmanship” are covered for one year. If your Parkway set is fairly new, check the warranty before you pay for replacements, and read the current page rather than relying on our summary.

## If a retailer can’t help

If the official route can’t supply what you need (a discontinued fabric, a single cushion, or a set bought second-hand), there are backups:

- **Aftermarket cushion makers.** CFR Patio in Pomona, California, which appears on Brown Jordan’s [Technician Locator](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/technician-locator), sells a “[Replacement Cushion Set for Brown Jordan Parkway Lounge Chair](https://cfrpatio.store/products/replacement-cushion-set-for-brown-jordan-parkway-lounge-chair)” at $399 (checked November 24, 2026). The listing says it is “not OEM manufactured,” includes a boxed seat and boxed back cushion with zippers and Velcro ties, and ships in 3–6 weeks. It publishes its own cushion sizes but doesn’t say which Parkway group’s lounge chair it fits, so compare its sizes with your measured cushions before ordering. Other shops, such as [Cushions USA](https://www.suncushions.com/manufactueres-brownjordan.html), list Parkway among the Brown Jordan collections they make cushions for.
- **Slings.** CFR Patio also lists a “[Replacement Parabolic Sling Kit for Brown Jordan Parkway Flat Adjustable Chaise w/ Wheels](https://cfrpatio.store/products/replacement-parabolic-sling-kit-for-brown-jordan-chaise-lounge)” at $583 (checked November 24, 2026), with a pre-sewn sling and gasket stripping and installation tools not included. Brown Jordan also lists a Parkway Sling chaise with wheels (5330-7800) in the catalog, so confirm which chaise yours is before you order.
- **Custom cushions from your measurements.** A custom shop can make loose seat and back cushions from your numbers. Tell them it is a Parkway Cushion or Modular piece on an aluminum frame, and send photos of how each cushion sits and attaches.
