Part of our [Brown Jordan guide](/brands/brown-jordan/). Palmetto is one of the newest Brown Jordan collections, and it is easy to confuse the two soft parts it uses: **tailored cushions** and a **cane-inspired sling**. Before you order anything, work out which one is actually worn.

## What Palmetto is

Brown Jordan introduced Palmetto at the Spring 2026 High Point Market. The launch press release (dated April 23, 2026, St. Augustine, FL) calls it an “11-piece seating collection” with “sculpted aluminum frames, tailored cushions, and a distinctive cane-inspired sling for dining, lounge, and poolside settings,” and says select pieces are also available as swivels ([High Point Market press release](https://medialink.highpointmarket.org/handlers/download.ashx?pressid=11036)).

The official [Palmetto collection page](https://www.brownjordan.com/products/collection/cushion/palmetto) describes the same three ingredients and adds two details that matter for replacements:

- “Cushions are detailed with an optional contrasting flange, adding definition and a tailored finish.”
- Frames come in all Brown Jordan powder-coat finishes, “with sling options including the new cane sling alongside the full range of existing sling materials.”

So a Palmetto chair is not always a cane-sling chair. Two sets bought from the same collection can have different sling materials and different cushion trims.

## Pieces listed on the brand site

Checked on November 19, 2026 (America/New_York), the collection page and Brown Jordan product pages listed pieces including the Armless Dining Chair, Arm Dining Chair, Swivel Arm Dining Chair, Armless Bar Stool, Armless Counter Stool, [Lounge Chair](https://www.brownjordan.com/palmetto-lounge-chair), [Swivel Lounge Chair](https://www.brownjordan.com/palmetto-swivel-lounge-chair), [Loveseat](https://www.brownjordan.com/palmetto-loveseat), [Sofa](https://www.brownjordan.com/palmetto-sofa), and [Ottoman](https://www.brownjordan.com/palmetto-ottoman). Search-index snapshots of the collection page also showed a high-back swivel lounge chair and chaise lounges (with and without wheels); the live page loads its product grid dynamically, so confirm the current list there rather than relying on ours.

## Cushion, cane sling, or both?

Use the parts you can see:

| What is worn | What it probably is | What to ask for |
|---|---|---|
| Faded, flattened, or torn seat or back pad that lifts off | A **tailored cushion** (possibly with a contrasting flange) | “Palmetto [piece name] replacement cushion,” plus fabric and flange fabric if you know them |
| Woven-looking panel stretched across the chair back or frame that does not lift off | A **sling** — the cane sling or another Brown Jordan sling material | “Palmetto [piece name] replacement sling,” plus the sling color |
| Both | Two separate parts | Order them as two line items |

The press release names three cane sling colorways: **Cane Desert, Cane Natural, and Cane Smoke** ([press release](https://medialink.highpointmarket.org/handlers/download.ashx?pressid=11036)). If your sling is a solid mesh rather than a cane look, it is likely one of Brown Jordan’s other sling materials; the brand’s site lists Versatex Sling among its textiles.

A sling is not a cushion pattern. A custom cushion shop cannot “re-cut” a sling, and a sling technician is not making a boxed or tailored cushion. Keep the two jobs separate, as Brown Jordan’s own FAQ does.

## The official replacement path

Brown Jordan’s [FAQ](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/faq) says:

- **Replacement cushions** come from your local Brown Jordan retailer ([store locator](https://www.brownjordan.com/locations)).
- **Replacement slings and strap** come from a Brown Jordan retailer or a local service technician.
- If you are unsure of the collection name, email a photo of the furniture and your name to `bjcservice@brownjordan.com`.

Because Palmetto launched in 2026, most sets are recent. If yours is still under warranty, read Brown Jordan’s current warranty and claims information before buying anything: the FAQ routes claims through a retailer with proof of purchase and photos.

## Furniture dimensions are not cushion sizes

Brown Jordan product pages publish furniture dimensions. For example, on November 19, 2026 the [Palmetto Sofa page](https://www.brownjordan.com/palmetto-sofa) listed W 81.5", L 34.75", H 36.5", seat height 19.5", and seat depth (SD) 20", and the [Palmetto Ottoman page](https://www.brownjordan.com/palmetto-ottoman) listed W 29.5", L 24.25", H 18". Those describe the **frame**, not the cushion. A seat-depth figure is not the depth of the pad you need, and a tailored cushion with a flange is not measured the same way as a plain box cushion.

If you go to a custom shop, measure the existing cushion itself (including where the flange sits and how much the pad overhangs the frame) with our [how-to-measure guide](/guides/how-to-measure/).

## What the official route costs (for context)

On November 19, 2026, Brown Jordan listed “Starting at MSRP” prices of $9,995 for the Palmetto Sofa and $2,295 for the Palmetto Ottoman ([Sofa](https://www.brownjordan.com/palmetto-sofa); [Ottoman](https://www.brownjordan.com/palmetto-ottoman)). Those are furniture prices, not cushion prices; Brown Jordan does not publish replacement-cushion prices online, so ask your retailer for a quote.

## Choosing fabric for a replacement

Brown Jordan says Palmetto’s tailored cushions are offered “in all fabrics” ([press release](https://medialink.highpointmarket.org/handlers/download.ashx?pressid=11036)). The brand’s textile menu lists Suncloth Fabric, Brown Jordan × Kravet, and Sien & Co | Sunbrella. If you want the replacement to match the rest of the set, ask the retailer for the exact fabric name used on your order — and for the flange fabric too, if your cushions have a contrasting edge. For general fabric trade-offs, see our [fabric guide](/guides/fabrics/).

## When a custom mill is the backup

If a retailer cannot supply a cushion (or you want a different fabric than the official list), a custom shop can make a cushion to your measurements. Tell them it is a tailored cushion with or without a contrasting flange, send photos of the corners and the zipper, and keep the sling out of that order.
