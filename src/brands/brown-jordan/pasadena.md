This page is part of our [Brown Jordan guide](/brands/brown-jordan/). **Pasadena** is a John Caldwell design for Brown Jordan, named after the city where the company was founded. Like [Fremont](/brands/brown-jordan/fremont/), it is sold under one name but in more than one build, and only one of them uses loose cushions. Before you ask anyone for “Pasadena cushions,” work out which Pasadena you own.

## One name, three builds

The official [Pasadena collection page](https://www.brownjordan.com/products/collection/multi-type/pasadena) (checked November 21, 2026) says the collection “pays homage to the sunny birthplace of Brown Jordan” and “is available as Pasadena Cushion and Pasadena Sling.” It describes them like this:

- **Pasadena Cushion:** “a smooth, flat extrusion of aluminum, gracefully curved arms and back legs, and impeccably tailored cushions.”
- **Pasadena Sling:** the armchairs and side chairs come “in Flex Sling, an innovative material that adjusts and conforms to the curves and movement of the body.”

Brown Jordan’s [All Collections catalog PDF](https://platform.brownjordan.com/media/pdf/All%20Collections%20Catalog.pdf) (the copy we downloaded is dated February 2024 in its file properties) splits the line into three groups, not two:

- **Pasadena Cushion** (catalog pp. 76–79)
- **Pasadena Padded Sling** (p. 82)
- **Pasadena Sling** (p. 83)

The Padded Sling pieces sit in the catalog’s Pasadena Sling section, and on brownjordan.com they’re also filed under “Pasadena Sling.” So a chair a retailer calls “Pasadena Sling” may be a plain sling or a padded sling. Neither one is a loose cushion.

A note on dates: our Brown Jordan guide quoted the All Collections web page (checked November 18, 2026) as saying Caldwell created Pasadena Cushion and Pasadena Sling “in 2017.” The catalog PDF says 2016 for Pasadena Cushion and 2015 for Pasadena Sling. The year doesn’t change how you order, but don’t rely on a purchase year alone to work out which version you have.

## Which Pasadena do you have?

### By the item number

If you have an invoice, packing slip, or spec sheet, the item number is the quickest check. In the catalog PDF and on the product pages we opened (checked November 21, 2026), each group uses its own prefix:

| Group | Item-number prefix | Pieces listed in the catalog |
|---|---|---|
| Pasadena Cushion | **5300-** | 5300-6000 Lounge Chair, 5300-5300 Swivel Motion Lounge Chair, 5300-6100 Ottoman, 5300-6200 Loveseat, 5300-6300 Sofa |
| Pasadena Padded Sling | **5240-** | 5240-2000 Arm Chair, 5240-1000 Side Chair, 5240-4600 Swivel Rocker, 5240-3500 Barstool with Arms, 5240-350B Counter Height Stool with Arms, 5240-5000 Lounge Chair, 5240-5300 Swivel Motion Lounge Chair, 5240-5100 Ottoman, 5240-7000 Stacking Adjustable Chaise |
| Pasadena Sling | **5190-** | 5190-2000 Arm Chair, 5190-1000 Side Chair, 5190-4600 Swivel Rocker, 5190-3500 Barstool with Arms, 5190-350B Counter Height Stool with Arms, 5190-5000 Lounge Chair, 5190-5300 Swivel Motion Lounge Chair, 5190-5100 Ottoman, 5190-7000 Stacking Flat Adjustable Chaise |

Pasadena tables use other numbers (5191-, 5192-, 5193-), so a table number won’t tell you which seating you have.

This prefix pattern is just what we saw in the current documents. Brown Jordan doesn’t present it as a rule, and older sets may have been numbered differently. If you’re not sure, use the photo route below.

### By the piece type

- **Only Pasadena Cushion has a loveseat and a sofa.** If your piece is a Pasadena loveseat or sofa, it’s almost certainly from the Cushion group. Confirm with the item number or with Brown Jordan.
- **Dining chairs, swivel rockers, and bar and counter stools appear only in the Sling and Padded Sling groups.** The catalog doesn’t list a Pasadena Cushion dining chair.
- **Lounge chair, swivel motion lounge chair, and ottoman appear in all three groups.** These are the pieces that get mixed up, so check the item number. The Pasadena Sling lounge chair and ottoman pages even say both pieces are “available in padded or sling.”

### By photo

Brown Jordan’s [FAQ](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/faq) (checked November 21, 2026) says you can email a photo of your furniture and your name to `bjcservice@brownjordan.com`, and the company “would be happy to provide you with the name of your Brown Jordan collection.” Ask them to confirm whether the piece is Pasadena **Cushion**, **Padded Sling**, or **Sling**.

## Cushion, padded sling, or sling?

| What is worn | What it probably is | What to ask for |
|---|---|---|
| A seat or back pad that lifts off the frame, faded, flattened, or torn | A **loose cushion** (Pasadena Cushion pieces) | “Pasadena Cushion [piece name] replacement cushions,” plus the fabric name if you know it |
| A padded seat-and-back panel that is fixed to the frame and doesn’t lift off | A **padded sling** (5240- pieces) | “Pasadena Padded Sling [piece name] replacement sling,” plus the color |
| Thin, tight mesh stretched across the frame, no padding | A **sling** (5190- pieces) | “Pasadena Sling [piece name] replacement sling,” plus the color |

Brown Jordan’s pages we checked don’t explain how a padded sling is built. So we can’t tell you whether it’s repaired as one part or two. What we can say is that independent sling shops treat it as a sling job, not a cushion job. For example, Patio Chair Supplies says on its [Brown Jordan replacement slings page](https://www.patiochairsupplies.com/brown-jordan-chairs-replacement-slings.html) that it makes “single layer slings for padded sling furniture (does not include a pad).” If you order a padded-sling replacement from anyone other than a Brown Jordan retailer, ask exactly what’s included.

The catalog also says Pasadena Cushion is “available with all Suncloth fabric and Versatex Sling.” Brown Jordan uses similar wording for [Fremont Cushion](/brands/brown-jordan/fremont/), whose collection page says it combines Versatex Sling mesh with loose cushions. The Pasadena pages we checked don’t say where (or whether) a sling sits on a Pasadena Cushion piece. So if something under or behind your cushions is worn too, photograph it and ask the retailer whether it’s a separate sling part.

## The official replacement path

Brown Jordan’s [FAQ](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/faq) (checked November 21, 2026) says:

- **Replacement cushions** “can be purchased from your local Brown Jordan retailer” ([Locations](https://www.brownjordan.com/locations)).
- **Replacement slings and strap** “can be purchased from a Brown Jordan retailer or a local service technician” ([Technician Locator](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/technician-locator)).

At least one retailer spells out more. Into the Garden, an outdoor-furniture retailer, says on its [Brown Jordan replacement cushions page](https://www.intothegardenoutdoor.com/brown-jordan-replacement-cushions/) (checked November 21, 2026) that Brown Jordan makes replacement cushions “for all CURRENT and CERTAIN DISCONTINUED COLLECTIONS,” and “also manufactures replacement cushions using customer’s own material.” For help identifying a collection, it asks for front, side, and back photos. Check with your own retailer, since policies can differ.

Brown Jordan doesn’t publish replacement-cushion or replacement-sling prices online, so you’ll need to ask a retailer for a quote.

## Furniture dimensions are not cushion sizes

Every Pasadena product page lists frame dimensions: width (W), length (L), height (H), seat height (SH), arm height (AH), and usually seat depth (SD). These describe the **furniture**, not the cushion. Two examples, checked November 21, 2026:

- The [Pasadena Cushion Lounge Chair](https://www.brownjordan.com/pasadena-cushion-lounge-chair) (5300-6000) page lists W 28.25", L 35", H 35", SH 18.5", AH 22.5", SD 21".
- The [Pasadena Padded Sling Lounge Chair](https://www.brownjordan.com/pasadena-padded-sling-lounge-chair) (5240-5000) page lists W 28.25", L 33.5", H 34.5", SH 16.5", AH 22.5", SD 21".

Both chairs list the same width and seat depth, but the frames are different: the cushion version is 2" taller at the seat and 1.5" longer. A matching SD figure doesn’t mean you can swap a pad from one to the other. Measure the cushion you actually have using our [how-to-measure guide](/guides/how-to-measure/). Because Brown Jordan calls these cushions “impeccably tailored,” photograph the corners, seams, and zipper too.

At those prices, replacing worn cushions or slings usually makes more sense than replacing the frame. Prices change, so check the live pages.

## Choosing fabric for replacement cushions

The catalog says Pasadena Cushion is “available in all finishes” and “available with all Suncloth fabric and Versatex Sling,” while both Pasadena Sling groups are “available in Versatex Sling.” The Padded Sling Dining Arm Chair page mentions “22 finish options” and “28 Versatex mesh sling choices.” Brown Jordan lists current colors on its [Suncloth Fabric](https://www.brownjordan.com/materials/textiles/suncloth-fabric) and [Versatex Sling](https://www.brownjordan.com/materials/textiles/versatex-sling) pages. Colors come and go, so if you want new cushions to match an older set, ask the retailer for the exact fabric name on your original order. For general fabric trade-offs, see our [fabric guide](/guides/fabrics/).

## If a retailer can’t help

If the official route can’t supply what you need (for example, a discontinued fabric, a single cushion, or a set bought second-hand), there are backups:

- **For loose cushions (Pasadena Cushion):** a custom cushion shop can make new ones from your measurements. Tell them they’re tailored loose cushions for an aluminum lounge frame, and send photos of the corners, seams, zipper, and how each cushion sits on the frame. Some independent shops say they already have Brown Jordan patterns. Cushions USA, for example, says on [its site](https://betterthanbrownjordan.com/) that it has “most Brown Jordan templates” and describes Pasadena cushions as a bullnose pattern. That’s the shop’s own claim. We haven’t verified it, so confirm the fit with them before you order.
- **For padded slings and slings:** use a sling maker, not a cushion shop, and confirm exactly what they include (see the table above).
