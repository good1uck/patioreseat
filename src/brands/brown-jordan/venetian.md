This page is part of our [Brown Jordan guide](/brands/brown-jordan/). **Venetian** is a long-running Brown Jordan collection, sold since 1987 and still in the current lineup. Unlike [Parkway](/brands/brown-jordan/parkway/), [Pasadena](/brands/brown-jordan/pasadena/), or [Fremont](/brands/brown-jordan/fremont/), it isn’t split into separate cushion and sling groups on the current site. The hard part with Venetian is different: many sets are decades old, retailers use their own names for the pieces, and some older pieces aren’t in today’s lineup.

## What Venetian is

Brown Jordan’s [Venetian collection page](https://www.brownjordan.com/products/collection/cushion/venetian) (checked November 24, 2026, America/New_York) says Richard Frinier designed the collection in 1987 and describes its “spiral volute details at the end of the arms, architectural banding on the legs, an x-back, cross-stretcher, and subtly flared and contoured back.” The brand’s [“80 Years of Timeless Design”](https://www.brownjordan.com/news/celebrating-80-years) article lists “Since 1987 – Venetian Collection.” The 2024 [All Collections catalog (PDF)](https://platform.brownjordan.com/media/pdf/All%20Collections%20Catalog.pdf) says the collection replicates “the hand-hammered, hand-forged look of heavy wrought iron in zero maintenance wrought aluminum.”

So if your set looks like wrought iron but is light enough to lift easily, Venetian is worth checking. The scroll ends on the arms and the X-shaped back are the details most people notice.

The collection page also says the Ritz Hotel in Paris worked with Brown Jordan on a Venetian collection for its garden terrace, with “French-welted cushions” chosen by architect Thierry Despont and a custom finish called Caper. That was a custom hotel order. Don’t expect a standard Venetian cushion from a retailer to look exactly like those photos.

The collection page itself links only some of these. The Right Arm and Center Armless sectional pieces appear as related products on the other sectional pages and in the catalog.

What Brown Jordan’s pages say about the cushions themselves is limited:

- The Dining Side Chair and Bar Stool pages describe fabric options “for the seated cushion,” so those take a seat cushion on the frame.
- The Loveseat and Sofa pages say each “includes 2 square pillows.” Those pillows are separate from the seat and back cushions.
- The catalog says Venetian is “available in all Suncloth fabric and Versatex sling” and that “contrasting welt” is available. As with [Parkway](/brands/brown-jordan/parkway/), Brown Jordan doesn’t say where a sling sits on a Venetian piece. Take the cushions off and photograph the frame before you order, and ask the retailer whether there is a sling under the cushions that also needs replacing.

## One piece, several names

This is where Venetian orders go wrong. Brown Jordan’s website, its own catalog, and its retailers all name the same pieces differently. On November 24, 2026:

| Brown Jordan website | Brown Jordan catalog | [Patio.com](https://www.patio.com/venetian) | [Patio Pete’s](https://patiopete.furnituredealer.net/collection/venetian-eb37b7e0f338/venetian) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Left / Right Arm Loveseat Sectional | Left / Right Arm Sectional | Venetian L/R Arm Seating - Sectional | Venetian Modular Left (or Right) Arm Loveseat |
| Corner Sectional | Corner Sectional | Venetian Corner Chair - Sectional | Venetian Modular Corner Chair |
| Lounge Chair | Lounge Chair | Venetian Lounge Chair | Scroll-Arm Lounge Chair |
| Dining Side Chair | Side Chair | Venetian Side Chair | X-Back Dining Side Chair w/Seat Cushion |

If a receipt says “Scroll-Arm Motion Lounge Chair” or “Modular Corner Chair,” it is almost certainly a retailer’s name for a Brown Jordan piece. Give the retailer the **2250- item number** if you have it. That number is the same everywhere.

Left and right matter on the sectional. The Left Arm (2250-6410) and Right Arm (2250-6420) pieces are listed with identical dimensions, and Brown Jordan’s pages don’t say which side “left” means. Don’t guess. Give the item number from your paperwork, or send a photo of the whole sectional taken from the front.

## Older Venetian pieces that aren’t on the list

Venetian has been sold since 1987, and not every piece made in that time is in today’s lineup. Aftermarket cushion shops list a **“Venetian Adjustable Chair”** with a hinged back-and-seat cushion ([Wicker Paradise](https://www.wickerparadise.com/venetian-adjustable-chair-cushions.html); [Replacement Cushions Online](https://www.replacementcushionsonline.com/brown-jordan-venetian-adjustable-chair-cushions.html)). We didn’t find an adjustable chair on brownjordan.com or in the 2024 catalog. The current site lists only the two adjustable chaises.

If your piece isn’t in the table above:

1. **Get it identified.** Brown Jordan’s [FAQ](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/faq) (checked November 24, 2026) says you can email a photo of your furniture and your name to `bjcservice@brownjordan.com`, and the company “would be happy to provide you with the name of your Brown Jordan collection.” Ask whether the piece is still made and, if so, its current item number.
2. **Ask a retailer next.** The same FAQ says replacement cushions “can be purchased from your local Brown Jordan retailer,” and that replacement slings and strap “can be purchased from a Brown Jordan retailer or a local service technician” ([Locations](https://www.brownjordan.com/locations); [Technician Locator](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/technician-locator)). Ask them whether they can still order cushions for a retired piece.
3. **If they can’t, measure and go custom.** See “If a retailer can’t help” at the end of this page.

## Furniture dimensions are not cushion sizes

Each Venetian product page lists the **furniture’s** dimensions: width (W), length (L), height (H), seat height (SH), arm height (AH), and seat depth (SD). From brownjordan.com on November 24, 2026:

| Piece | Item | W | L | H | SH | SD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dining Side Chair | 2250-1000 | 22.5" | 23" | 35" | 20" | 18" |
| Dining Arm Chair | 2250-2000 | 22.75" | 23.25" | 35" | 20" | 18" |
| Bar Stool | 2250-3500 | 17.5" | 19" | 43.5" | 31" | 15.5" |
| Lounge Chair | 2250-6000 | 28.25" | 32.75" | 34" | 19" | 21" |
| Motion Lounge Chair | 2250-5300 | 28" | 33" | 34" | 19" | 21" |
| Ottoman | 2250-6100 | 27.25" | 24.25" | 19" | 19" | — |
| Adjustable Chaise | 2250-7000 | 27.25" | 78" | 40" | 19" | 48" |
| Adjustable Chaise w/Wheels | 2250-7700 | 31.25" | 78" | 40" | 19" | 48" |
| Loveseat | 2250-6500 | 54.25" | 32.75" | 34" | 19" | 21" |
| Sofa | 2250-6300 | 80" | 33" | 34" | 19" | 21" |
| Left / Right Arm Loveseat Sectional | 2250-6410 / -6420 | 53.75" | 32.75" | 34" | 19" | 21" |
| Center Armless Loveseat Sectional | 2250-6430 | 53.75" | 32.75" | 34" | 19" | 21" |
| Corner Sectional | 2250-6440 | 32.75" | 32.75" | 34" | 19" | 21" |

Here is why you shouldn’t work out a cushion size from this table:

- **The sources disagree on small things.** The website lists the sofa at 80" wide and 33" long. The catalog prints 80.25" wide and 32.75" deep. The catalog prints the Venetian square pillow (2250-6300-C100) as 20" × 20" on the Venetian page and as 21" × 21" with corded welt on its pillow page.
- **Seat depth isn’t cushion depth.** Brown Jordan lists the lounge chair’s seat depth as 21". [Replacement Cushions Online](https://www.replacementcushionsonline.com/brjovelochre.html) lists its own Venetian lounge chair seat cushion as 28" wide and 26" deep (checked November 24, 2026). We can’t tell you which number matches your chair. The point is that a published seat depth is a measurement of the frame, not of the cushion.

Measure the cushions you have with our [how-to-measure guide](/guides/how-to-measure/), and photograph the corners, the welt, the zipper, and any ties.

## Fabric and warranty notes

The catalog says Venetian is available in all Suncloth fabric. Brown Jordan’s [Suncloth Fabric](https://www.brownjordan.com/materials/textiles/suncloth-fabric) page shows the current colorways, and the product pages offer a separate welt fabric choice. If you are matching cushions you already have, ask the retailer for the fabric name and number from your original order rather than matching by eye, and say whether your cushions have a contrasting welt. Our [Softscape guide](/brands/brown-jordan/softscape/) explains Suncloth Fabric vs. Suncloth Strap, and our [fabric guide](/guides/fabrics/) covers outdoor fabrics in general.

Brown Jordan’s residential warranty on its [Claims & Warranty page](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/claims-and-warranty) (checked November 24, 2026) covers “Fabric on Cushions, Slings, Umbrellas and Furniture Covers” for 5 years (limited) if the fabric “becomes unserviceable due to color or strength loss from normal usage and exposure conditions.” Failures in “stitching, zippers, sling rails, cording, buttons, cushion foam or other areas of workmanship” are covered for one year. If your Venetian set is fairly new, check the warranty before you pay for replacements, and read the current page rather than relying on our summary.

## If a retailer can’t help

If the official route can’t supply what you need (a retired piece, a discontinued fabric, a single cushion, or a set bought second-hand), several aftermarket shops make cushions they describe as fitting Venetian pieces. None of them is Brown Jordan. Prices below were checked November 24, 2026 and will change:

- **[Replacement Cushions Online](https://www.replacementcushionsonline.com/brjoverecu.html)** (Mount Vernon, New York) lists a Venetian lounge chair seat-and-back set at $412 and a Venetian adjustable chair set at $395, both shipping in 3–4 weeks.
- **[Wicker Paradise](https://www.wickerparadise.com/venetian-adjustable-chair-cushions.html)** lists a Venetian adjustable chair set (back cushion “25 Inches Wide, 53 Inches Long (Hinged Cushion), 4 Inches Thick”) at $479, also 3–4 weeks. It uses the same item number (457) as the Replacement Cushions Online listing.
- **[ZipCushions](https://zipcushions.com/pages/brown-jordan-replacement-cushions)** lists Venetian cushions for several pieces. Its [lounge chair seat-and-back set](https://zipcushions.com/products/cushions-made-to-fit-brown-jordan-venetian-lounge-chair-replacement-cushions) was $571.07.

Before you order from any of them, compare the shop’s published sizes with your measured cushions, not with Brown Jordan’s furniture dimensions. Most of these are made to order and may not be returnable, so check the shop’s return policy first. A general custom shop can also cut cushions to your measurements. Tell them it is a Brown Jordan Venetian piece on an aluminum frame, and send photos of how each cushion sits and attaches.
