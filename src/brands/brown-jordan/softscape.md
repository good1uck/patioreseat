This page is part of our [Brown Jordan guide](/brands/brown-jordan/). **Softscape** is a John Caldwell design for Brown Jordan. Like [Fremont](/brands/brown-jordan/fremont/) and [Pasadena](/brands/brown-jordan/pasadena/), it is sold under one name in more than one build, and only one of them uses loose cushions. It also brings a second word that confuses owners: **Suncloth**, which Brown Jordan uses both for a cushion fabric and for a woven strap.

## One name, two builds

Brown Jordan’s [Softscape collection page](https://www.brownjordan.com/products/collection/multi-type/softscape) (checked November 22, 2026, America/New_York) describes “elegant silhouettes, plush seating, classic lines, and modern aluminum construction” and lists two groups: **Softscape Cushion** (5 products) and **Softscape Strap** (7 products). The collection description on the same site says: “John Caldwell’s 2018 Softscape Cushion and Softscape Strap collections debuted our Suncloth fabric, a next generation tech material that conforms to the body while keeping elasticity.” Of the strap group, the page says it “showcases resilient Suncloth straps hand woven on shapely frames.”

A 2018 launch article in *Arizona Foothills Magazine* reported that the collection came in “22 Brown Jordan custom frame finishes, 12 Suncloth strap colors and more than 150 exclusive outdoor fabrics for cushions” ([Arizona Foothills Magazine](https://www.arizonafoothillsmagazine.com/living/decor/9231-brown-jordan-debuts-2018-luxury-outdoor-furniture-collections-just-in-time-for-fall.html)). Treat those counts as a 2018 snapshot; color menus change.

## Which Softscape do you have?

### By the item number

If you have an invoice, packing slip, or spec sheet, the item number settles it. On the product pages we opened (checked November 22, 2026), each group uses its own prefix:

| Group | Item-number prefix | Pieces listed on brownjordan.com |
|---|---|---|
| Softscape Cushion | **5350-** | 5350-6000 Lounge Chair, 5350-5200 Motion Lounge Chair, 5350-6100 Ottoman, 5350-6200 Loveseat, 5350-6300 Sofa |
| Softscape Strap | **5340-** | 5340-2000 Dining Arm Chair, 5340-1000 Dining Side Chair, 5340-3500 Bar Stool, 5340-5000 Stacking Lounge Chair, 5340-5200 Motion Lounge Chair, 5340-5100 Ottoman, 5340-7200 Chaise (described as a stacking adjustable chaise with wheels) |

This prefix pattern is what we saw on the current pages. Brown Jordan doesn’t present it as a rule, and the list may not include every piece ever made in the line.

### By the piece type

- **Only Softscape Cushion has a loveseat and a sofa.** If yours is a Softscape loveseat or sofa, it is almost certainly from the Cushion group.
- **Dining chairs, the bar stool, and the chaise appear only in the Strap group** on the current site.
- **The lounge chair, motion lounge chair, and ottoman appear in both groups.** These are the pieces that get mixed up, so check the item number. The frames are not the same: for example, the Cushion Lounge Chair page lists a 34" width and an 18" seat height, while the Strap Stacking Lounge Chair page lists 29.75" and 16".

### By photo

Brown Jordan’s [FAQ](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/faq) (checked November 22, 2026) says you can email a photo of your furniture and your name to `bjcservice@brownjordan.com`, and the company “would be happy to provide you with the name of your Brown Jordan collection.” Ask them to confirm whether the piece is Softscape **Cushion** or Softscape **Strap**.

### Don’t look for it in the 2024 catalog

Brown Jordan’s [All Collections catalog PDF](https://platform.brownjordan.com/media/pdf/All%20Collections%20Catalog.pdf) (the copy we downloaded is dated February 2024 in its file properties) has no Softscape section. Its table of contents lists collections such as Fremont Cushion, Pasadena Cushion, Stretch, and Still, but not Softscape. The brownjordan.com product pages were still live, with prices, when we checked. One retailer, Patio 1 in Houston, labels its Softscape Cushion listings “a discontinued collection” and says “availability may be limited” ([Patio 1](https://patio1.com/soft-scape-deep-seating/)). Brown Jordan’s own pages we checked don’t say that, so ask your retailer whether replacement cushions in your fabric can still be made.

## Cushion or strap? What is actually worn

The Softscape Cushion product pages don’t only offer a fabric choice. Their configurators (checked November 22, 2026) show **Frame Finishes**, **Fabrics**, **Straps** (the example shown is “White Suncloth Strap”), and **Welt** on the lounge chair, motion lounge chair, loveseat, and sofa. The ottoman page shows Fabrics and Welt with no strap color picked. So on most Softscape Cushion seating, there is a Suncloth strap on the frame *and* loose cushions. Brown Jordan’s pages don’t show exactly where the straps sit, so photograph the piece with the cushions removed before you order anything.

| What is worn | What it probably is | What to ask for |
|---|---|---|
| A seat or back pad that lifts off the frame: faded, flattened, torn, or a failed zipper | A **loose cushion** (5350- pieces) | “Softscape Cushion [piece name] replacement cushions,” plus your fabric name, number, and welt choice if you know them |
| Woven bands on the frame that are broken, faded, or mildewed | A **Suncloth strap** (5340- pieces, and the strap on most 5350- pieces) | A strap replacement for the exact piece, plus the strap color |

Brown Jordan’s FAQ treats these as different jobs. Replacement cushions “can be purchased from your local Brown Jordan retailer,” while replacement slings and strap “can be purchased from a Brown Jordan retailer or a local service technician” ([FAQ](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/faq); [Locations](https://www.brownjordan.com/locations); [Technician Locator](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/technician-locator)). A cushion shop is the wrong place for a broken strap, and the reverse.

## Suncloth: fabric vs. strap

Brown Jordan sells two different Suncloth products, on two different material pages:

- **[Suncloth Fabric](https://www.brownjordan.com/materials/textiles/suncloth-fabric)** — the upholstery menu for cushions. The page we checked on November 22, 2026 showed named colorways such as Revival Onyx, Form Onyx, Floris Azure, Cortina Cotton, and Organic Sage, many marked “New.”
- **[Suncloth Strap](https://www.brownjordan.com/materials/textiles/suncloth-strap)** — the woven strap. On November 22, 2026 the page listed Pebble, Camel, Slate, Sea, White, Spa, Mica, Coal, and Marine.

The Brown Jordan pages we checked don’t publish Suncloth’s fiber content, so we don’t state it here. What helps more for a replacement order is the **number on your original fabric**. In the February 2024 catalog, Suncloth fabrics are listed with a four-digit number, a name, and a letter grade in parentheses — for example “1553 COTTON (A)” and “1575 ALABASTER (A)” — and across the Suncloth fabric pages we counted grades A, B, C, and E. Suncloth straps are listed by number and width, such as “510 WHITE (1”)” and “511 WHITE (2”).” The catalog doesn’t cover Softscape itself, so we can’t tell you which strap width Softscape uses. Ask the retailer to look up your original order, and give them the fabric number and grade if you have them. A name alone like “Cotton” or “White” can match more than one product.

For general fabric trade-offs (solution-dyed acrylic, polyester, olefin), see our [fabric guide](/guides/fabrics/).

## Cleaning and warranty notes that affect replacement

Brown Jordan’s [Care and Maintenance page](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/care-and-maintenance) (checked November 22, 2026) gives separate instructions for the two materials:

- **Performance fabrics (cushions):** clean “with mild soap and water using a soft cloth or brush, rinse thoroughly, and air dry standing upright.” For mildew or deep stains, use a quality commercial outdoor furniture cleaner. “Do not dry clean, machine wash, tumble dry, or use steam above 100°F.”
- **Suncloth strap & lace:** brush off debris, use a mild warm soap-and-water solution, rinse and air dry, blot spots from the outside inward, and “do not rub and do not use stain removers.” Brown Jordan adds that sunscreen and suntan products “can permanently stain Suncloth® materials; such stains are not covered under warranty.”

The residential warranty text on Brown Jordan’s [Claims & Warranty page](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/claims-and-warranty) (checked November 22, 2026) covers **fabric on cushions** for 5 years (limited) against becoming “unserviceable due to color or strength loss,” but says failures in “stitching, zippers, sling rails, cording, buttons, cushion foam or other areas of workmanship” are covered for one year, and it excludes labor and installation costs. For **Suncloth strap and lace**, it says a broken strap within 5 years, or one that fades or mildews within two years, gets a replacement “sent directly to the client.” Softscape launched in 2018, so if your set is fairly new, ask about the warranty before you pay for a replacement. Claims go through Brown Jordan’s customer service and need the applicable written warranty, so read the current page rather than relying on our summary.

## Furniture dimensions are not cushion sizes

Every Softscape product page lists frame dimensions: width (W), length (L), height (H), seat height (SH), arm height (AH), and usually seat depth (SD). These describe the **furniture**, not the cushion. From brownjordan.com, checked November 22, 2026:

| Piece | W | L | H | SH | SD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Cushion Lounge Chair](https://www.brownjordan.com/softscape-cushion-lounge-chair) (5350-6000) | 34" | 37" | 34" | 18" | 23" |
| [Cushion Loveseat](https://www.brownjordan.com/softscape-cushion-loveseat) (5350-6200) | 55" | 37" | 34" | 18" | 23" |
| [Cushion Sofa](https://www.brownjordan.com/softscape-cushion-sofa) (5350-6300) | 78" | 37" | 34" | 18" | 23" |
| [Cushion Ottoman](https://www.brownjordan.com/softscape-cushion-ottoman) (5350-6100) | 32" | 19" | 15" | 17" | — |

Retailer listings don’t always agree. Patio 1’s page for the same Softscape Cushion pieces lists the lounge chair at 33.5" wide, 36.5" high, with a 19" seat height ([Patio 1](https://patio1.com/soft-scape-deep-seating/)). That is one more reason not to work out a cushion size from a spec table. Measure the cushion you actually have with our [how-to-measure guide](/guides/how-to-measure/), and photograph the corners, the welt, and the zipper.

## If a retailer can’t help

If the official route can’t supply what you need — a discontinued fabric, a single cushion, or a set bought second-hand — there are backups:

- **For loose cushions (Softscape Cushion):** a custom cushion shop can make new ones from your measurements. Tell them they are loose seat and back cushions with welt for an aluminum frame that also has woven straps, and send photos of how each cushion sits. Owners do ask whether they can get Brown Jordan–style foam from a custom maker; one Reddit commenter in r/BuyItForLife said they were “searching for companies who offer the same material in a custom fabrication” ([Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/BuyItForLife/comments/1chccn9/patio_furniture_foam_cushions/)). Ask any shop exactly which foam and fabric they would use.
- **For straps (Softscape Strap, or the strap under your cushions):** use a Brown Jordan retailer or a service technician, per the FAQ, not a cushion shop.
