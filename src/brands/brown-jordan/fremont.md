This page is part of our [Brown Jordan guide](/brands/brown-jordan/). Brown Jordan sells **Fremont** as two related collections designed by John Caldwell: **Fremont Cushion** and **Fremont Sling**. The frames look alike, but the soft parts are different. A retailer will need to know which collection you have before they can quote a replacement.

## Two collections under one name

The official [Fremont collection page](https://www.brownjordan.com/products/collection/multi-type/fremont) describes the two collections this way:

- **Fremont Sling** “unites timeless Neoclassical design influences with 100% solution dyed Versatex Sling mesh.”
- **Fremont Cushion**: “John Caldwell’s sophisticated cushion design includes Versatex Sling mesh and tailored loose cushions that conform to the frame and embrace the body.”

That second line matters. On the brand’s own description, a Fremont Cushion piece uses **both** Versatex Sling mesh **and** loose cushions. A Fremont Sling piece uses only the mesh. So owning a Fremont Cushion chair doesn’t mean every worn part on it is a cushion.

The same page says Versatex Sling “is made by dipping polyester threads in vinyl coating, then stretching the threads by machine to the maximum give.”

## Which Fremont do you have?

### By the item number

If you have an invoice, packing slip, or spec sheet, check the item number. In Brown Jordan’s current catalog and product pages (checked November 20, 2026), the two collections use different prefixes:

| Collection | Item-number prefix | Examples from brownjordan.com |
|---|---|---|
| Fremont Cushion | **4340-** | 4340-6000 Lounge Chair, 4340-5200 Motion Lounge Chair, 4340-6100 Ottoman, 4340-7000 Adjustable Chaise, 4340-6200 Loveseat, 4340-6300 Sofa, 4340-6460 Curved Sofa |
| Fremont Sling | **4320-** | 4320-2000 Dining Arm Chair, 4320-1000 Dining Side Chair, 4320-3600 Arm Bar Stool, 4320-360B Swivel Counter Height Stool with Arms, 4320-5000 Lounge Chair, 4320-4600 Swivel Rocker, 4320-5100 Ottoman, 4320-7000 Adjustable Chaise |

Sources: the [All Collections catalog PDF](https://platform.brownjordan.com/media/pdf/All%20Collections%20Catalog.pdf) (Fremont Cushion on pp. 22–25, Fremont Sling on pp. 26–29) and the individual product pages listed under Sources. Fremont tables use other prefixes (4321-, 4323-, and so on), so a table number won’t tell you which seating collection you have.

This prefix pattern is just what we saw in the current documents. Brown Jordan doesn’t present it as a rule, and older sets may have been numbered differently. If anything is unclear, use the photo route below.

### By the piece type

Some pieces only appear in one collection on the current site:

- **Only in Fremont Cushion:** Motion Lounge Chair, Loveseat, Sofa, Curved Sofa.
- **Only in Fremont Sling:** Dining Arm Chair, Dining Side Chair, Swivel Rocker, Arm Bar Stool, Swivel Counter Stool.
- **In both:** Lounge Chair, Ottoman, Adjustable Chaise. These are the pieces that get mixed up, so check the item number or ask Brown Jordan.

### By photo

Brown Jordan’s [FAQ](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/faq) says you can email a photo of the furniture and your name to `bjcservice@brownjordan.com`, and the company “would be happy to provide you with the name of your Brown Jordan collection.” Ask them to confirm whether the piece is Fremont **Cushion** or Fremont **Sling**.

## Cushion, sling, or both?

| What is worn | What it probably is | What to ask for |
|---|---|---|
| A seat or back pad that lifts off the frame, faded, flattened, or torn | A **loose cushion** (Fremont Cushion pieces) | “Fremont Cushion [piece name] replacement cushions,” plus the fabric name if you know it |
| Tight mesh stretched across the frame that doesn’t lift off | A **Versatex sling**, found on Fremont Sling pieces and, per Brown Jordan, also part of the Fremont Cushion design | “Fremont [Cushion or Sling] [piece name] replacement sling,” plus the sling color |
| Both | Two separate parts | Order them as two line items |

A custom cushion shop can’t make a sling, and a sling technician doesn’t make loose cushions. Treat them as two separate jobs. Brown Jordan’s FAQ splits them the same way.

## The official replacement path

Brown Jordan’s [FAQ](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/faq) (checked November 20, 2026) says:

- **Replacement cushions** “can be purchased from your local Brown Jordan retailer” ([Locations](https://www.brownjordan.com/locations)).
- **Replacement slings and strap** “can be purchased from a Brown Jordan retailer or a local service technician” ([Technician Locator](https://www.brownjordan.com/support/technician-locator)).

Brown Jordan doesn’t publish replacement-cushion or replacement-sling prices online, so you’ll need to ask a retailer for a quote.

## Furniture dimensions are not cushion sizes

Every Fremont product page lists frame dimensions: width, length, height, seat height (SH), arm height (AH), and usually seat depth (SD). These describe the **furniture**, not the pad. Two examples from November 20, 2026:

- The [Fremont Cushion Lounge Chair](https://www.brownjordan.com/fremont-cushion-lounge-chair) page lists W 34", L 35.25", H 32", SH 19", AH 24", SD 23".
- The [Fremont Sling Lounge Chair](https://www.brownjordan.com/fremont-sling-lounge-chair) page lists W 30", L 30.75", H 32.25", SH 16", AH 23", SD 21".

The two lounge chairs have different frame sizes. So even if you know the size of one, you can’t use it to work out a cushion or sling for the other.

Published figures can also disagree. On the Fremont Cushion Curved Sofa, the [product page](https://www.brownjordan.com/fremont-cushion-curved-sofa) lists H 32", but the catalog PDF lists H 48.5" for the same item, 4340-6460. That’s one more reason to measure your actual cushion. Our [how-to-measure guide](/guides/how-to-measure/) explains how. For a loose cushion that “conforms to the frame,” photograph its corners and edges too.

## What new Fremont pieces cost (for context)

On November 20, 2026, brownjordan.com listed these “Starting at MSRP” furniture prices. They’re prices for whole pieces, not replacement parts:

- Fremont Cushion: Lounge Chair $3,695; Motion Lounge Chair $4,695; Ottoman $2,295; Adjustable Chaise $5,895; Loveseat $6,745; Sofa $8,995; Curved Sofa $9,795.
- Fremont Sling: Dining Arm Chair $1,695; Dining Side Chair $1,595; Lounge Chair $2,495; Ottoman $1,595; Swivel Rocker $2,895; Arm Bar Stool $2,695; Adjustable Chaise $3,595.

These prices show why repairing a Fremont frame usually makes more sense than replacing it. Prices change, so check the live pages.

## Choosing fabric for replacement cushions

The catalog says Fremont Cushion is “available in all finishes” and that its cushions are “available in all Suncloth fabric and Versatex Sling.” Fremont Sling is listed as “available in Versatex Sling.” Brown Jordan lists the current [Suncloth Fabric](https://www.brownjordan.com/materials/textiles/suncloth-fabric) and [Versatex Sling](https://www.brownjordan.com/materials/textiles/versatex-sling) colors on its materials pages. Colors come and go, so if you want new cushions to match an older set, ask the retailer for the exact fabric name on your original order. For general fabric trade-offs, see our [fabric guide](/guides/fabrics/).

## If a retailer can’t help

If the official route can’t supply what you need (for example, a discontinued fabric, a single cushion, or a set bought second-hand), there are two backups:

- **For loose cushions:** a custom cushion shop can make new ones from your measurements. Tell them they’re loose, tailored cushions for an aluminum lounge frame. Send photos of the corners, the zipper, and how the cushion sits on the frame. Leave the sling out of that order.
- **For the mesh:** independent sling makers exist. For example, Patio Furniture Supplies offers [custom replacement slings for Brown Jordan models](https://www.patiofurnituresupplies.com/slings-cushions-umbrellas/brown-jordan-slings.html) and asks owners to email a side photo so it can confirm the model. We haven’t checked whether its patterns fit Fremont, so confirm fit with them before you order.
