Unlike some outdoor manufacturers that route every pad through a local dealer, Crate & Barrel sells many official replacements on its own website. The listings are titled **“Replacement [collection] …”** and are written as companions to a specific frame (the furniture is typically sold separately).

If you still know the collection name, search that name plus “replacement cushion” on [crateandbarrel.com](https://www.crateandbarrel.com/). If you do not, use photos and our [measuring guide](/guides/how-to-measure/) rather than guessing from a sofa’s overall width.

## Official replacements we opened

The two product pages below were opened on November 18, 2026 (America/New_York). Prices and color assortments change; the live product page is the authority. Construction notes are quoted or closely paraphrased from those pages only.

### Batten dining chair

[Replacement Batten Canvas Sunbrella Outdoor Side Chair Cushion](https://www.crateandbarrel.com/replacement-batten-canvas-sunbrella-outdoor-side-chair-cushion/s589423) is “designed especially for our Batten outdoor dining chair.” On the day we checked, the page listed **$135.00**.

The details section states:

- An all-weather Sunbrella cushion cover “made with solution-dyed acrylic fibers to be fade-resistant, water-repellent and stain-resistant”
- A seat cushion with a **polyfoam insert** and an **underside ventilation zipper**
- **Made in USA of Imported Materials**

Color names shown on that page included Canvas, Black, and Cast colors such as Silver, Shale, Teak, and Harbor. Confirm the current assortment on the product page before you order, and match it to any remaining pieces in the set.

### Mallorca sofa

[Replacement Mallorca Canvas White Sunbrella Outdoor Sofa Cushion](https://www.crateandbarrel.com/replacement-mallorca-canvas-white-sunbrella-outdoor-sofa-cushion/s518752) is a cushion set for the Mallorca outdoor sofa (“sold separately”). On the day we checked, the page listed **$1,399.00**.

The details section states:

- **100% acrylic** weather-resistant fabric
- **Polyfoam core** cushions with poly-fiber fill and wrap
- **Texteline drainage panels**
- **Waterfall construction**
- Topstitching
- **Made in Indonesia**

The page offered a wide color list, including Canvas White, Natural, Canvas, Flax, Java, Navy, Beach Ivory, and several Cast colors. Again, use the live page for what is actually in stock.

We did **not** copy overall furniture dimensions from these pages as cushion sizes, because the details we saw describe materials and construction, not a published width × depth × thickness for the foam.

For more on what “solution-dyed acrylic” means, see the [fabric guide](/guides/fabrics/).

Match the **exact piece type** (dining chair vs. lounge chair vs. sofa vs. chaise vs. left/right-arm chair vs. loveseat sectional). A Mallorca sofa cushion is not a Mallorca lounge-chair cushion.

## If the official SKU is gone

Retailers retire collections. If the replacement page 404s, take measurements ([guide](/guides/how-to-measure/)), keep photos of the frame and any leftover fabric, and consider a custom mill. Ask the mill to copy construction you can document — for example, a vented underside zipper or a waterfall front — rather than inventing a thickness.
