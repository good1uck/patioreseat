PatioReseat is a small, independent English-language website about **patio furniture replacement cushions**. The aim is simple: help you keep a sound frame, measure what you actually have, and find either an official replacement or a custom one — without invented sizes or invented specifications.

This is version 1. It is not a store. We do not sew cushions, and we do not stock fabric.

## What we publish

- Practical guides, starting with [how to measure](/guides/how-to-measure/) and [outdoor fabrics](/guides/fabrics/)
- Brand pages that explain **how that manufacturer says you should replace cushions**, plus named collections we can verify on a public page
- Source links on every researched page

We do **not** publish cushion dimensions unless a primary source states them as cushion sizes. Overall furniture width, depth, and height from a catalog are not cushion sizes, and we will not treat them as such.

## Independence

PatioReseat is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Brown Jordan, Homecrest Outdoor Living, Crate & Barrel, Sunbrella (Glen Raven), Cushion Mill, Covers & All, Amazon, or any other brand mentioned here. Brand names and fabric names are used to identify products people search for. All trademarks belong to their owners.

## How we source facts

Brand and fabric statements on this site are taken from public manufacturer or retailer pages and documents, which we link. Prices, program rules, and in-stock lists change. Where we mention a price, we date the check. If a page later changes, the live manufacturer page wins.

Collection lists are snapshots of what the official site showed when we checked, dated on the brand page. They are not a complete historical catalog.

## Affiliate disclosure

Some links on PatioReseat may become **affiliate links**. If you follow one and later buy something, we may receive a commission. It does not change the price you pay. We will only recommend a path that we would also describe without a commission — official factory or retailer replacements first when they exist, custom mills when they do not, and measurements in either case.

**Current status (November 2026):** affiliate applications have not been attached to this site yet. Any partner URL is a **clearly marked placeholder** with no tracking ID. Those placeholders are labeled “Affiliate link — placeholder” and use `rel="nofollow sponsored"`. Until real IDs are added, a click is just a visit to the merchant’s public website.

When partner accounts are approved, we will replace the placeholders with tracked URLs and keep this disclosure in the site footer and on this page.

If you have a question about a disclosure or a factual error, use the contact line below once a public inbox is set up. Until then, treat this preview as a working draft.
